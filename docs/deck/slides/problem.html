In 1994, at the Milford Haven refinery, two operators faced 275 alarms in the last eleven minutes before an explosion: one every two to three seconds. The alarm that mattered had sounded 25 minutes earlier. There is no evidence anyone saw it. Today, plants want AI copilots in that room. But an AI that sounds right and is wrong is just one more alarm.
